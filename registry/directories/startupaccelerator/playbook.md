# StartupAccelerator

- Open /submit and sign in or sign up with Google; the draft stays on the page.
- The form is a project directory and community launch board. Choose a category and immediate need.
- Required: name, one-line pitch, story (200–5,000 characters), website, category, need, square logo (64–2,048 px, max 1.5 MB), and OG thumbnail (minimum 600 × 315 px, max 5 MB). Enter the website to auto-fill both images.
- Optional: YouTube video or upload (MP4/WebM/MOV, max 25 MB), Reddit username, first comment, founder email. Reddit members may receive priority review, but this is optional.
- Free listing is manually reviewed, usually within 24 hours. Paid sponsored placement is optional. A Featured-on badge is an optional post-publication perk.
