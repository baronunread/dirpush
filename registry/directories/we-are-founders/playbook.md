# We Are Founders

Use the linked Tally form (https://tally.so/r/PdvzgQ), not email. Select Standard for free or Fast-track for $49. Free queue is 4–6 weeks. The form requires a one-line tagline up to 120 characters, 1–3 categories, founder stage, pricing, 3–5 specific differentiators, honest poor-fit use cases, email, and a social profile; submission adds the address to their Friday Brief newsletter.

It also has a required field "Where have you linked to We Are Founders?": give the URL of a page on your site that links back to the directory. Put that link live first. Needs backlink permission.
