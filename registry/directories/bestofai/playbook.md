# BestofAI

- No dedicated /submit or /add URL (both 404) — use 'Add Tool' or 'Submit a Tool' buttons on the homepage.
- Sign-in modal (Clerk-powered) offers 'Continue with Google' or email/password + 'Sign up' link.
- Fill tool details (name, description, categories, etc.) after auth, upload logo and screenshots.
- No badge/backlink required at any step to submit or go live.
- Optional: 'Sponsor Your Tool — $250/mo' for featured placement (separate from listing itself).
