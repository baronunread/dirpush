# AINave

- Submit at https://ainave.com/submit: enter the product URL and continue through Main info, Images and media, then Pricing and links.
- URL import may not populate the fields; verify and enter product name, tagline and description yourself (name limit 40, tagline limit 60).
- Upload a square PNG/JPG/WebP thumbnail (up to 3 MB) and up to 5 screenshots (up to 3 MB each). Use current approved assets.
- Choose pricing type, up to 3 categories, and supported social links. The final control is “Submit for review”. Authentication may be requested at that point; login page offers Google. Never select a paid upgrade.
- Free tier is $0 forever with no strings - public listing, community discovery. Nothing asks the submitter to embed a badge anywhere.
- Optional $5 one-time 'Verified' upgrade (paid via Dodo Payments) adds a verified badge, guaranteed do-follow backlink, higher visibility, and future 'Launchpad' eligibility. Pricing page copy is a bit inconsistent (Free tier's bullets also mention 'Verified badge'/'Do-follow backlink'), but nothing requires action from the submitter either way.
- Live listings at /products/{slug}, e.g. /products/retypewords - but some /products/ URLs are category pages (saas, developer-tools, etc.), not individual listings.
- The observed form supports a thumbnail and up to 5 screenshots; the previous registry entry incorrectly said screenshots were unsupported.
