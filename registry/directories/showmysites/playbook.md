# ShowMySites

- Broad site/product directory (SaaS, blogs, marketplaces, AI tools, apps), not niche.
- Sign up with Google or email; you also pick a username at signup, which becomes part of your profile/listing URL (showmysites.com/{username}/{slug}/) - not derivable from the product name.
- Free tier lists up to 25 active sites per account with no badge/backlink requirement to be listed at all; the badge/reciprocal-link only upgrades the outbound link from nofollow to dofollow.
- $10 one-time (per account, not per listing, not a subscription) Premium removes the reciprocal-link/badge condition entirely and adds a first-listing homepage 'Founder's Choice' spotlight plus custom anchor text.
- Payments processed via Paddle.
