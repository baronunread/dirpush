# ScrollLaunch (formerly reached via solofounders.info)

- solofounders.info now 301s straight to www.scrolllaunch.com; there is no separate SoloFounders site to submit to at that domain.
- Sign-in is required before creating a listing (Google OAuth or email magic link at /auth/signin); /dashboard/products/new redirects to sign-in when logged out.
- IMPORTANT: despite pricing-page copy suggesting the free tier 'goes live' with just a dofollow caveat, a real submission showed the product actually stays in Draft/Unverified state with 'Verification Required: Add our badge to your website to verify ownership and unlock publishing' until the ScrollLaunch badge is embedded on the submitter's site. Budget for adding the badge if you want the free listing to actually go live.
- Free plan also caps the current week's homepage leaderboard at 20 free listings.
- Live listings are at /products/{slug}.
- Site also publishes a curated 'Best startup directories' list (1,018+ entries) ranked by Ahrefs Domain Rating - a possible source for further harvesting.
