# 10015.io

- Submit at https://10015.io/product-finder/submit (NOT /submit, which now 404s).
- Step 1: paste product URL, click 'Submit URL' - this triggers auto-fetch of name/description/logo.
- Immediately after, a 'Sign In / Register' modal blocks further progress. Only social providers are offered: Google, Twitter, GitHub, Facebook, Twitch, Discord - no email/password. An account is required to finish the submission (pick categories/tags, submit).
- Free tier: standard review queue 3-4 months, nofollow link, no badge required to go live.
- Paid upsells: $9.99 one-time priority review (live in 24h); $2.99+/month featured (dofollow backlink from high-DA domain, Google indexing, top placement, featured badge) - optional, not required for the free listing.
- Listing pages live at /product/{slug} (e.g. /product/anyvids-ai).
- To update a listing later, email contact@10015.io.
