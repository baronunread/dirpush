# KittyLaunch

- /pricing now works and lists 3 launch tiers plus a-la-carte add-ons: FREE LAUNCH ($0, queue-based, 7 days on homepage, permanent listing, dofollow backlinks only if you verify a homepage badge), Skip the line ($9, no badge needed, pick your date), SuperKitty ($29, skip line + tools + Spotlight ticket). Separate upsells: Spotlight (from $15), Highlight ($9/mo), Review ($29).
- Submission starts at /new/product (reachable from the homepage 'Get Started Free' button) and is a 3-step wizard: Core Details -> Launch Settings -> Engagement & Deals. You can fill Website URL, click 'AI Autofill', and enter Product Name/Category/Tagline/SEO description/Logo/Pricing before hitting a login wall.
- Login is Google OAuth or email+password (real password field, not a magic link).

- Live product pages sit at https://kittylaunch.com/p/{slug} (confirmed via 'backlink-guard').
