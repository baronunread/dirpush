# SubmitHunt

- /submit requires sign-in via Google only (no email option in the login modal).
- Free tier is genuinely optional-badge: you launch regardless, badge just upgrades your backlink from nofollow to dofollow - not a listing requirement, reconfirmed on this recheck.
- Free plan: live on homepage 7 days, editorial review, launch delayed ~1 week.
- Premium ($20/launch): skip queue, 14 days live, guaranteed dofollow with no badge, newsletter feature.
- Featured Spot ($50 one-time): premium feed placement for 7 days.
- Daily launch format with countdown timer to next launch slot; ranked by votes.
- Product cards on the homepage/feed link straight to each product's own external site, not an internal listing page - no predictable listing_url_pattern.
