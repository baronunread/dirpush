# DodoDirectory

- /submit is a 3-step wizard: 1) choose plan, 2) details, 3) publish.
- Free plan ($0): requires backlink + a badge that must remain on your site forever, or you get delisted; ~2 week review; listing capped ~3,000 characters, no AI SEO description.
- Premium ($9.99 one-time): no backlink/badge required, instant approval, ~6,000-character AI-written listing.
- Very broad directory (943 listings) covering not just SaaS/AI but local business, food, health, etc.
