# AlternativeTo

- Sign-up at https://alternativeto.net/signup has an hCaptcha and then email verification, so a new account needs the human: fill the form, block with --type captcha so they tick it in the agents' Chrome, then block for the email verification.
- Once logged in, the submit form is at /software/add. Fields beyond that were not seen.
- Wait for manual review (can take months).
- Optional: Pay for priority review to move to front of queue (~1-2 business days). Do not pay.
- Becoming a verified member also gets automatic fast-track (free)
- Apps listed on https://alternativeto.net/browse/new-apps/ once approved
