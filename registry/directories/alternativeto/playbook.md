# AlternativeTo

- Sign up with email at https://alternativeto.net/signup
- Once logged in, go to /software/add to submit your app
- Fill in app details and wait for manual review (can take months)
- Optional: Pay for priority review to move to front of queue (~1-2 business days)
- Becoming a verified member also gets automatic fast-track (free)
- Apps listed on https://alternativeto.net/browse/new-apps/ once approved
