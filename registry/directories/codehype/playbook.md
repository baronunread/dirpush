# CodeHype

- Public form: https://www.codehype.in/submit. The page asks for the website first and says AI fills in product details. Name (30 chars), tagline (140 chars), website, and category are visibly required; description is optional (up to 10,000 characters).
- The long form has optional tags, logo and screenshot uploads, target audience, problem/solution/differentiation prompts, tech stack, and social links.
- Free organic submissions receive human review in the standard queue. The free-tier do-follow link remains while the maker’s badge is installed.
- Paid Plus is $15 one-time for instant publish and a do-follow link without a badge.
- The site asks the maker to sign in when ready to upload media and launch; methods were not visible from the public form. No submission was sent.
