# The Startup Project

- Submit through https://startupproject.org/submit-startup/; no account or sign-in prompt is shown.
- Complete founder name, email, company name, website, tagline, industry, stage, monthly revenue, team size, and startup description. Traction & milestones is optional.
- The checkbox requires agreement to keep a backlink to the listing on the startup website. Submission does not guarantee publication. The team says it reviews submissions within a few business days. The optional Featured badge is not required.
