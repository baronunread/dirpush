# ShinyLaunch

- Weekly launch-board format (Latest/Winners nav tabs, tagline 'new products from innovative makers every week'), not a static permanent directory - same template family as LaunchClash.
- /submit and /sign-up both offer Google only, no email/password option anywhere.
- No pricing page or paid tier exists; homepage runs a third-party ad for a 'submit to 140+ directories' service, unrelated to ShinyLaunch itself.
- Form fields not visible pre-auth.
