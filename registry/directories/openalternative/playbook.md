# OpenAlternative

- Only for genuinely open-source tools (positions itself as "open source alternatives to popular software"); don't use for closed-source SaaS.
- /submit redirects to /auth/login?next=/submit. Sign in via 'Send me a Magic Link' (email), 'Continue with Google', or 'Continue with Github'. The form asks for project name, website URL, and repository URL; suggested alternative and discount fields are optional.
- After submitting those details, the site requires a paid package to finalize: Standard $97 (48h), Premium $137 (24h), or Ultimate from $197/month. No free listing option was shown. Do not proceed unless the spend permission changes; the current launch permission is never.
- The separate 'Advertise' page is site-wide banner advertising and is unrelated to product listing.
- Live listings sit at the bare slug https://openalternative.co/{slug} (e.g. /n8n, /supabase, /immich) -- use this to verify a submission went live once approved.
