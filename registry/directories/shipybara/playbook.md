# Shipybara

- Sign in with Google or GitHub at /submit
- Free tier: 18 weekly slots, featured homepage placement
- Dofollow backlink available only if product ranks top 3 or you display a Shipybara badge
- Premium plan ($50/mo) guarantees backlink without conditions
- Launches happen every Monday at midnight UTC
- Can schedule submissions up to 180 days in advance (free) or 30 days (premium)
