# StackLedge

- `/submit` redirects signed-out visitors to login. The email/password signup path is available; confirming the email unlocks commenting and listing contact, but the wizard opened before confirmation.
- Choose “Tool or product” and use the URL-first fetch. Review every generated field against product facts; remove unsupported category or pricing claims.
- The form accepts a logo upload and up to five screenshots. Free Standard is $0 and nofollow. Featured is $9 for 30 days; the site says payment is due only after approval. Never choose Featured or pay.
- The free path currently places the listing around 24 days in the human-review queue, then requires three public upvotes on other listings before the submit step. Do not cast these votes for the user; ask them to choose and perform them.
- The form mentions an included badge, but does not ask the submitter to embed it. No backlink from the product site is required for the free listing.
