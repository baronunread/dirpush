# SaaSBison

- /submit is a 3-step wizard; step 1 shows pricing before any account/login prompt was hit.
- Free Listing ($0 forever): 1 dofollow backlink, badge required on your site, slow manual review, shorter (~1,500 char) AI-written listing, no priority support.
- Premium Listing ($9.99 one-time, normally $29.99): no badge, instant approval, 3 dofollow backlinks, longer (~3,500 char) listing, priority support.
- Separate $149 add-on (via partner LaunchDirectories) submits to 60+ other directories.
- Separate sitewide sponsored ad banner ($30/30 days, no auto-renew) sold via /ad/setup — distinct from the directory listing itself.
- Checkout via Polar (merchant of record); no crypto/adult/gambling/illegal content allowed, non-refundable if in violation.
