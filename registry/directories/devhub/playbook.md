# DevHub

- Sign in at https://devhub.best/sign-in (Google, GitHub or email) before /projects/submit.
- Free launch needs the DevHub badge live on your site with a verified backlink, and Ahrefs DR >= 5.
- Free queue is up to 60 days; Premium $9.90 skips it.
- Daily launches at 8:00 AM UTC, ranked by votes; top 5 weekly get newsletter, top daily gets an X post.
- Form fields not visible without an account.
