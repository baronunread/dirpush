# Toolfio

- /submit is a step wizard: 1) Choose plan, 2) Fill details, 3) Submit & Pay.
- FREE LISTING: gated by Domain Rating check (needs DR 20+ via Ahrefs) entered before you can fill in any details; also requires a permanent 'Featured on' badge with backlink on your own site -- bots crawl regularly and removing the badge causes automatic delisting. This makes the free tier CONDITIONAL, not unconditional.
- PREMIUM LISTING ($18.99 one-time): no badge, no DR requirement, 3 permanent backlinks, instant approval, AI-written SEO listing.
- REVIEW ARTICLE ($39.99 one-time): everything in Premium plus an in-depth ~10k-char SEO review article published as a blog post.
- No login/signup wall observed before the DR-check step.
