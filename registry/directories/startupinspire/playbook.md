# StartupInspire

- The old /submit URL redirects to the homepage. Go to /pricing and click 'Submit now' under 'Free Submission' to reach signup.
- Signup form (email/password only, no Google/GitHub): Email (business email), Business Name, Password, Confirm Password, accept Terms checkbox.
- Free tier (EUR0): 1 startup, instant approval, permanent listing, 'featured badge and highlight' (displayed on their site, not an embed you add to yours), homepage visibility -- same perks as paid tiers, just capped at 1 startup.
- Standard EUR19/lifetime: up to 5 startups. Premium EUR49/lifetime: unlimited, plus promo banner and featured homepage header.
- No badge/backlink requirement anywhere on pricing or FAQ to submit or go live.
- Trustpilot 4.7/5 (105 reviews), 15,000+ startups submitted.
