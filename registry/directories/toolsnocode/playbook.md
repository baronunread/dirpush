# ToolsNoCode

- Requires an account before you can submit; 'Get Started' opens /signup with real email+password (with confirm-password) or 'Continue with Google' — no magic link.
- Standard listing is free, ordered by date added, no badge/backlink strings attached at any step.
- $49.90/yr 'Boost' upgrade (optional) buys a full-width featured card at the top of the directory, category pages and homepage, a demo video slot, a boosted badge, and better search ranking — this badge is a paid perk shown on the listing, not a submission requirement.
- Live tool pages sit at https://toolsnocode.com/tools/{slug} (confirmed via 'cognira').
