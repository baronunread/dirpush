# Cursor Marketplace

- Submit at https://cursor.com/marketplace/publish (linked from the marketplace "Publish" button and from cursor.com/docs/plugins: "submit it for review at cursor.com/marketplace/publish").
- A plugin can bundle MCP servers, skills, rules and hooks. Package it as a Cursor plugin (manifest per the plugins reference at cursor.com/docs/plugins; the repo can use `.cursor-plugin/marketplace.json` for multi-plugin repos).
- Requirements from the Publisher Terms: open source under a permissive license (no GPL/AGPL/LGPL), accurate metadata, free for users, visible terms of service and privacy policy, follow Cursor brand guidelines.
- Cursor says every plugin is manually reviewed and every update is reviewed before publishing. Review can take weeks; forum threads report submissions pending with no reply.
- The form sends no automatic confirmation; the "Thanks for applying" screen means it went through. Do not resubmit (creates a duplicate). After later changes, request a re-index rather than a new application.
- Conflict: a July 2026 forum reply from a Cursor team member says the old marketplace form is generally no longer accepted and cursor.directory is now the main listing location, while an October 2026 reply and the Terms still point to the form. Not established which is authoritative; cursor.directory sits behind a Vercel challenge (429) and could not be read, so no separate entry was created.
- Listing pages link out to the publisher site (e.g. https://cursor.com/marketplace/adspirer/adspirer) with `rel="noopener noreferrer"` and no robots meta.
- Research only; no account created or listing submitted.
