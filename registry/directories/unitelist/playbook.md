# Unite List

- Navigate to https://unitelist.com/submit.
- Must log in first: only 'Log in with Google' / 'Sign up with Google' are offered, no email/password option.
- Submit flow is 3 steps: Website > Badge > Details. The Details step (where you actually fill in the listing) only unlocks after Unite List verifies a do-follow badge linking back to your listing is present on your product's homepage or footer - same badge-gate template as Startup Benchmarks.
- No pricing tiers or paid upsell found anywhere on the site; homepage FAQ confirms use of the directory (browsing/submitting) is free, subject to manual review - but the badge is a hard requirement to reach the submission form, not optional.
- Listing pages live at /product/{slug} (e.g. /product/labrynth-ai).
