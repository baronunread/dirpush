# Website Rating

- No account needed; /submit/ is a public form. Enter the bare homepage URL: the field rejects sub-pages and URLs with UTM query parameters ("Please enter your homepage ... not a link to a specific page") and it auto-fetches title/description/preview image to pre-fill.
- Category dropdown: AI, Developer Tools, Marketing, Design, Productivity, Business, No-Code, Founder Tools, Ecommerce.
- A Cloudflare Turnstile checkbox sits on the form itself (routine captcha, not a wall in either headless or headed mode observed).
- Free queue is reviewed in 6-10 weeks and its link is nofollow and approval is not guaranteed (spam/illegal/adult excluded, otherwise 'every genuine startup gets listed'). Content must be in English; only one submission per domain.
- No badge/backlink required on the submitter's own site at any step.
- $49 Fast Track publishes in 1-2 business days with a dofollow link (the free queue link is nofollow). $99 adds homepage/category featured placement. $149 adds a published founder interview.
- Live listings sit at https://www.websiterating.com/startups/{slug}/ (confirmed via 'mapgrid').
