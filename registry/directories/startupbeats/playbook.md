# StartupBeats

- Submit a Startup profile from `/submit`; signup uses email and password and the free account includes one listing.
- In the six-step wizard, choose Startup, enter the product URL for editable AI autofill, then review every field against canonical facts. The autofill may invent details such as founding year, funding stage, or team size; clear any unsupported values.
- The free publish option is explicitly labeled “Submit for review (Free).” Featured placement starts at $29/month and is optional; do not select a paid plan.
- The company profile supports a logo, cover image, and multiple product screenshots. Human review usually takes within 24 hours; the public profile appears after approval.
