# TrueRev

- Open /submit; unauthenticated visitors redirect to /login?next=%2Fsubmit.
- Google and email/password sign-in are available.
- Listing is free and publishes immediately; paid promotions are separate.
- Five steps: Basics, Story, Business, Revenue & sale, Review.
- Logo: square PNG/JPG/WebP, minimum 64×64, maximum 2 MB. No screenshots field in this flow.
- Use canonical launch date; leave unknown founding date, revenue and growth blank. Keep the for-sale switch off unless explicitly authorized.
- Freemium model; optional pricing, starting price and target audience. Review all details before Publish product.
- Public listing URL: https://www.truerev.in/startup/{slug}.
- Verification is separate: Stripe read-only restricted key, encrypted by the directory. Do not connect billing or enter unverified revenue. The public verification guide describes self-reported listings.
- Check the public search for the product before submitting.
