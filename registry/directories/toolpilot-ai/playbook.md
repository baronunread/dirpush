# ToolPilot AI

- Submit at https://www.toolpilot.ai/pages/submit-your-ai-tool (embeds a Jotform, no account needed to submit).
- Long form: personal details, tool details (name/URL/tagline/description/overview/up to 5 features), a huge integrations checklist (pick what applies, none required), pricing/subscription details, social accounts, marketing/affiliate fields.
- At the end, 'Choose your listing type': Free With Backlink, Priority ($99 one-time), Premium ($199 then $39/mo).
- Free tier requires adding a ToolPilot badge/logo to your own site and providing that backlink URL (or emailing to confirm) — this is a real condition, not just an upsell.
- No stated review time for the free tier (Priority/Premium both claim live within 7 days).
- Listing pages live at /products/{slug} (e.g. /products/sunoh-ai).
