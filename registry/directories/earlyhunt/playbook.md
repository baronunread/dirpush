# EarlyHunt

- AI-products-only launch platform, same template/family as IndieHunt (they cross-link as alternatives).
- Free tier requires displaying the EarlyHunt badge on your own site; link is nofollow by default, becomes dofollow only if you win top-3 that week.
- $19 one-time Premium Launch removes the badge requirement, guarantees dofollow, skips the queue, and adds a Launch Kit (9 graphics) plus social promotion across X/LinkedIn/Bluesky/Pinterest.
- Launches run weekly, Monday 8:00 AM UTC.
