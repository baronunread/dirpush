# BuiltByMe

- Submit Product routes to `/login`; signup is available from the Login/Sign Up tabs.
- Login offers Google or email/password. Submission fields are not visible before sign-in.
- Free tier has up to five weekly launch slots and a permanent product page with a nofollow link.
- Optional $9 launch skips the queue and includes a dofollow link; $19 Sponsor buys 30-day placement. Do not choose paid plans without explicit authorization.
- Product pages use `/products/{slug}`; a live example is `/products/absencely`.
