# CtrlAltShip

- Submit Your Product opens a public form; no account creation is needed before filling it. The form says the required email will create or link an account.
- Required: email, product name, URL, one-sentence tagline (max 70 characters), full description, main features, up to three categories, at least one replaced big SaaS product, pricing model and description, founder name, simple arithmetic anti-spam answer, and terms acceptance.
- Optional: differentiator, square logo, up to six screenshots (8 MB each), new competitor suggestion, founder Twitter, and co-founder details.
- The site says automated review checks whether the product is live, indie, and solves a problem; accepted listings go live within a few days. Free with no upsell.
