# Joinly

- Submit a startup or product through https://joinly.xyz/submit-startup; no account is required.
- Required: name, website URL (domain only, no path or parameters), email, headline (up to 80 characters), country, thumbnail image, and terms/privacy agreement. Long description and gallery images are optional.
- Thumbnail recommendation is 256 × 256 px, max 200 KB. Up to three gallery images are accepted, recommended 1024 × 768 px and max 400 KB each. After Submit, the site redirects to a paid checkout ("Featured for 7 days", EUR 5.59). Whether the free listing is received is unclear, so do not assume the submission is free.
