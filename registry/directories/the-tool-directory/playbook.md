# The Tool Directory

- Open https://thetooldirectory.com/submit-a-tool/. No account is needed.
- A Funding Choices cookie-consent dialog covers the submit button; dismiss it first.
- The "Prefill from website" button can fill the form from the product URL.
- Logo is a URL field, not an upload.
- Leave the paid featured-placement checkbox unticked. The growth-challenge radio group is optional.
- "Submit" is the final free submit. Success shows ?submitted=1 and "submitted for review".
