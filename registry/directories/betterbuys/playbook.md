# BetterBuys

- No account or login needed - it's a plain contact-style form at /for-ai-companies/.
- Fields: product name, product website, vendor/company name, contact email, one-line description (max 160 chars, plain English on what it does and for whom), primary category (huge AI-focused dropdown, e.g. 'AI Agent Builders', 'AI for Healthcare & Life Sciences').
- Page states detailed paid listing packages are still being finalized - current submission is free, they'll follow up later about paid options. No badge/backlink requirement anywhere.
- No autofetch, no logo/screenshot upload.
- Also offers a separate 'Claim your listing' link for products already listed elsewhere on the site.
