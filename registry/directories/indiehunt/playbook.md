# IndieHunt

- AI products only (site framed entirely around 'AI project' submissions and 'Weekly AI launches for indie makers').
- Free tier requires the IndieHunt badge/backlink live on your site; the project link itself is nofollow by default and only becomes dofollow if you place top-3 in weekly voting.
- $19 Premium Launch drops the badge requirement, guarantees dofollow, adds a Launch Kit (9 graphics), social promotion across X/LinkedIn/Bluesky/Pinterest, and priority review.
- Launches run weekly, Monday 8:00 AM UTC cohorts.
