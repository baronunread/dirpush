# LaunchBoard

- Free submissions are limited to 10 slots per week and require the LaunchBoard badge embedded on the product website. Confirm the submitter is willing to add the badge before proceeding.
- Without the badge, the visible prices are $19 for one launch or $39 for five. Paid launches include a do-follow backlink; the free tier offers one only to the weekly #1 winner.
- The linked submission page is https://www.launchboard.dev/dashboard/submit. It showed the dashboard navigation and a Sign In button while signed out; no fields or auth methods were visible.
- Pricing page: https://www.launchboard.dev/pricing.
