# Startup List

- Needs the human for sign-up and sign-in. On /register and /login, Create Account and Sign In send no network request under browser automation and the page stays put (checked 2026-11-01). Do these by hand in the human’s own browser, then continue.
- Login supports Google or email/password; registration at /register asks for name, email, and password, plus a required newsletter-consent checkbox.
- Open /submit once signed in. Search for the product before submitting. The multi-step form asks for product name, website URL, short description (10-200 characters), optional long description and logo, then category, pricing model, optional tags, referral link, pros, and cons.
- Upload accepts PNG, JPG, GIF, or WEBP up to 2 MB. Screenshots are not supported in the form.
- Select the accurate pricing model and category.
- The free standard listing is available. On the review step, the £10 boost for 3-day placement is checked by default; uncheck it before submitting. Do not choose paid upgrades. Optional Featured plan is £49/week.
- The site states standard submissions are usually reviewed within 24-48 hours. The form says submit for review; this is not immediate publication.
