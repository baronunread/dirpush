# InventList

- Search the public catalog first, then open `/submit/new`. A free account is required; the sign-in page offers Google, GitHub, and X. Google requests identity-only scopes (`openid`, `email`, `profile`).
- Enter the product URL first, then provide a title (20-character max), tagline (200-character max), category, primary category, and optional short description (300-character max).
- The free submission remains private until approved. Optional sponsorship is paid. The optional InventList badge is not needed for a listing, but is required for search, trending, and newsletter reach; do not add it when backlink permission is `never`.
