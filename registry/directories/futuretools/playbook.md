# FutureTools

- No account required; submit directly at futuretools.io/submit-a-tool
- Fill form with tool name, URL, description, category (AI-related categories), pricing model, and email; a Cloudflare Turnstile widget sits on the form itself
- Manual review by Matt Wolfe; expect ~1 week response time; most submissions are rejected on editorial curation grounds
- Common rejection reasons: duplicate concept, low-effort website/product, not publicly available, adult content, custom GPTs, or poor reviews
- If not approved within a week, can resubmit (wait at least a week)
- No badge/backlink required at any step to submit or go live
- Paid placements (featured listing max 2/week, site banners, newsletter sponsorship) have no published prices - email matt@futuretools.io for a quote
- Listing URLs include a random hash suffix (e.g. /tools/cognira-6fbe56e5), can't be predicted from the product name
