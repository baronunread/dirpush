# SpacerrApps

- Start at the homepage URL field, then submit an app website to enter the wizard; later fields require the wizard and were not opened.
- Free listing requires a badge on the product site, verified in the dashboard, plus upvoting three other apps.
- Free launch waits until the first Monday at least four weeks after approval.
- Paid launches start at $15 and bypass the badge and delay. Write-ups are no longer included in free listings (policy changed Sep 28, 2026).
