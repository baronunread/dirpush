# Toolsfine

- AI tools directory for discovery and comparison.
- Submission is paid-only: $16 for basic listing, $50 for featured placement.
- Fill out submission form with tool name, website, company, email, pricing model, description, and logo URL.
- Stripe payment required; no free tier available.
- Editorial review takes 3 business days (weekends excluded).
- Approved tools published to /best-ai-tools/{slug}.
- Featured listings displayed on homepage and most tool pages.
