# eubuilt.eu

- Public form: https://eubuilt.eu/submit. It asks for product name, URL, tagline (100 chars), 150–300-word description, category, non-EU alternatives, legal HQ country/city, privacy policy URL, and submitter name/email/relationship.
- Submission is free, with manual editorial review usually within a week. Every listing is re-checked every six months.
- Strict requirements: EU legal HQ, no conflicting foreign law/CLOUD Act exposure, and documented GDPR compliance with public privacy policy and DPA.
