# CodeTrendy

- /submit asks you to create a free account first (Google, GitHub, X, LinkedIn, Discord or email); the form unlocks afterwards and says it checks what it fills in from your link.
- Free path: place the CodeTrendy badge on your homepage at least 7 days before it goes live; free listings show as Unverified and take about 7 days to review.
- Paid plans ($19/$39/$99 one-time) publish instantly with no badge.
- Form fields not visible without an account.
