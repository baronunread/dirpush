# BrowserExtension.dev

- Only for browser extensions (Chrome/Firefox/etc), not general SaaS/web products.
- Submission is entirely through a 3-page Google Form (forms.gle/9XsqpoUSLeL6ZzW47), not a form on the site itself. No login required to fill it (a 'sign in to save progress' offer is optional). No badge/backlink requirement anywhere.
- Page 1: required Yes/No newsletter opt-in question. Page 2: required 'Your Name', optional 'Twitter Link' and 'Blog/Website'. Page 3 (extension details) not seen — stopped before filling required fields per research rules.
- Site also runs maker interviews as content/promotion, separate from the plain submission form.
- Listings live at https://browserextension.dev/directory/{slug}/ once accepted (curated, not automatic).
