# SaaS.fyi

- Submit at https://saas.fyi/submit. The public form asks for product name, website, category, pricing model, tagline (about 100 characters), description, tags, founder name, and email.
- Free is $0 but requires the Featured on SaaS.fyi badge on the site within 7 days of featuring; the agreement says the listing may be archived if the badge is removed.
- Premium is $99 one-time and waives the badge. Do not select it under current spend permissions.
- A person reads every submission; most take 5–7 days.
- Existing listings show an example URL such as https://saas.fyi/tools/orb.
