# Turbo0

- Sign up at turbo0.com/submit (email + password, email verification). The submit flow has 3 steps: the listing form, then the plan choice, then verification.
- Form: category, tags, an Introduction of at least 1200 characters, a Description, and an image (16:9 preferred, max 1MB; a square logo is accepted). Step 1 creates a draft item in "Submitting" status.
- FREE PATH: requires Ahrefs DR above 10 and a backlink on your site. "Verify DR & badge first" on the plan page checks both. A link to plain https://turbo0.com fails ("Badge or exact item link not found on your website"). It needs the badge (`<a href="https://turbo0.com/item/{slug}"><img src="https://img.turbo0.com/badge-listed-light.svg" …></a>`, dark variant also offered) or a link to the exact item page `https://turbo0.com/item/{slug}`. Add that link before verifying. 3 dofollow links, reviewed within 2 weeks.
- PAID PATHS:
  - Basic ($29.9 one-time): Remove backlink requirement, immediate listing, customizable links
  - Pro ($99.9 one-time): All basic + featured listing forever, 5 customizable links
  - Review Blog ($199 one-time): Dedicated SEO article on Turbo0 blog
- All plans include listing to directory with backlinks (DR 80+)
