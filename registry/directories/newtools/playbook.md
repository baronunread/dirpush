# NewTools

- Mkdirs-based site; sign in with email/password, Google or GitHub at https://newtools.site/auth/login.
- Submit form at https://newtools.site/submit is a 3-step flow: Details, Payment (first step shows "First Free"), Publish.
- Details: Link (with "AI Autofill" button), Name, Categories, Tags, Description, Introduction (Markdown), Icon 1:1 PNG/JPEG max 1MB, Image 16:9 PNG/JPEG max 1MB.
- Free plan requires adding a backlink to the directory on your site; Pro ($9.9 one-time) skips it.
- Not checked: exact review time beyond what pricing states. Free reviewed and listed within 72 hours, choose publish date. Listing sample https://newtools.site/item/saascity.
