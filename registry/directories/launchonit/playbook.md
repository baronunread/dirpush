# LaunchOnIt

- Public submission form: https://launchon.it/submit?week=40. It is usable as a guest; signing in is optional and attaches the launch to the maker profile. The form says drafts auto-save locally.
- Start with product URL and click Fill with AI. Required visible fields include product name, tagline (50 chars), URL, use cases (up to 5), and a logo. Description is up to 2,000 chars; screenshot, video, and launch date are optional.
- Free plan uses the standard queue and provides a permanent product page and a seven-day launch week. Weekly capacity is 20; choose an available future week if the current board is full.
- Launch costs $19 one-time for guaranteed placement, priority, social shoutout, and do-follow link without a badge. Growth is $39 one-time and adds a review article and backlinks.
