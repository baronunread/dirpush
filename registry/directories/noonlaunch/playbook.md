# NoonLaunch

- Submit at https://noonlaunch.com/submit. It redirects to Google sign-in; signing in creates an account. No password option. Product fields are hidden until authenticated.
- Standard is free forever and joins the weekly launch queue. The high-authority backlink is tied to keeping the badge live.
- Premium costs $11 once per product: skips the queue, pins the launch, removes the badge requirement, and includes a list of 200+ do-follow backlinks. Spotlight is $49 once per product and includes Premium benefits plus a written review.
- No form fields or media support were visible signed out. No submission was sent.
