# ToolTrove

- Go to https://tooltrove.net/submit
- Enter tool name, website URL, category and email; continue to the details step.
- Add the one-line summary, a full description (at least 100 characters), optional audience/problem, pricing and up to 8 key features.
- The final step requires a logo (PNG/JPG/WebP, up to 5 MB), at least one screenshot (up to 4), and Cloudflare Turnstile. Use approved current assets and leave the visible Turnstile for human completion if it fails.
- Submit for human review (typically 2 days)
- Once approved, can claim listing to edit and earn Verified badge
- Claim also allows replying to user reviews
