# Built Here

- EU-focused launchpad for European indie products.
- Free plan includes a permanent product page, community upvotes, maker profile, and nofollow link; launch enters a queue.
- During beta, Premium is also displayed as free (€0; stated regular price €29) and adds a dofollow link, badge, chosen date, and three-day homepage spotlight.
- Sign in at /login via GitHub or email/password; signup link is available. Submission form is hidden until sign-in.
