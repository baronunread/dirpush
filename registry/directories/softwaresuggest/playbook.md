# SoftwareSuggest

- Old submission_url /add-a-product now 404s. Use nav 'Add a Product' from homepage, which lands on https://www.softwaresuggest.com/vendors ('List Your Product - It's Free').
- Form asks for: last name, business email, company, phone (country-code selector), product URL, company head office, number of employees, number of customers, software/service type, category name, and competitors.
- No login needed to start; account created via this flow (email-based).
- Paid plans ($4k/$9k/$15k, plus smaller add-ons) are B2B vendor packages for featured placement, reviews, interviews and marketing support - not required for a basic free listing, and no badge/backlink is required to go live.
