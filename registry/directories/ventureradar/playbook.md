# VentureRadar

- Open Submit a Company and choose Get started.
- Create a free VentureRadar account to submit a company profile. The signup form exposes email and password fields plus first and last name; terms mention registration by LinkedIn as an alternative.
- The site says submitted profiles are verified by the VentureRadar team and, if accepted, go live within 21 business days. Premium Profile is an optional paid route to instant verification. The submission form fields appear after account creation and were not visible during read-only research.
