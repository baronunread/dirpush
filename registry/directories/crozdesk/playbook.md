# Crozdesk

1. From crozdesk.com click 'Apply to list your software' / 'Become a Vendor' -- both go to https://vendor.revleads.com/user/signup ('Free Revleads Account Application', explicitly no credit card required).
2. Fill contact details (name, email, **phone required**), company details (name, HQ country), and software details: product name, software URL, one primary category (secondary categories added later), a **175-character** description, and a **required** 'Monthly Marketing Budget' dropdown -- there's no way to skip this field even for the free tier.
3. Submission goes through an automated eligibility check ('advanced machine learning bots' per the form's own text) rather than an obviously manual queue.
4. Once approved you get vendor-portal access to manage the free crozdesk.com/software/{slug} listing and can add more products/team members there.
5. Crozdesk pitches 'Premium Listings' (sponsored content + deep links) and display ads as separate paid add-ons throughout (banner on vendor-policy page, Revleads knowledge base) -- decline to stay on the free tier.
6. No email/password login was reached in this session; auth is bundled into the application itself, so 'email' is the only confirmed method (no Google/GitHub/social login seen).
