Startup Ranking: free global startup directory ranked by 'SR Score'. Visiting /create-startup/ triggers a Cloudflare check (passes headed after ~8s wait), then redirects to /login - no email/password signup, only social login (Google, Facebook, LinkedIn, X/Twitter). Per /how-it-works: after creating the listing it goes to manual approval, stated as taking up to 80 days, unless you pay $49 for SR Faster Approval (24hr). No badge/backlink requirement anywhere - the free listing goes live on its own, just slowly. Other paid upsells: SR Booster (submits to 120+ other directories), SR Premium Profile, SR Advertising. No logo/screenshot upload seen pre-login.
