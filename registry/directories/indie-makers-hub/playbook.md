# Indie Makers Hub

- Open /submit/ and click Submit Your Product.
- Submission uses an embedded Google Form and requires Google sign-in; the form fields were not visible before sign-in.
- The listing is free and the site states review takes 2-3 business days.

- The submit form (https://indiemakershub.com/dashboard/submit/) requires a 1280x720 thumbnail and a logo.
