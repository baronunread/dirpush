# Linkrena

- Submit is public and does not require an account.
- The URL-first form asks for product URL, name, a one-line description, and optional email for listing notification/claiming.
- Pricing states that listings are free forever and each submission enters the weekly launch round. A top-three finish earns a badge; no badge is needed for listing.
- Paid visibility options are separate: skip the line $4.99, featured $29/7 days, homepage spotlight $39/30 days, or Maker Pro $9/month ($79/year).
