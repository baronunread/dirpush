# Launch List

- The free path uses the community launch queue. The pricing FAQ says makers must upvote three other products launching in the same week.
- A Launch List badge must be placed on the product site to receive the free do-follow backlink. The listing itself is presented as a free community submission.
- Optional paid placements: Featured ($19, 7 days) and Premium ($39, 14 days). These are one-time payments, not subscriptions.
- The free CTA routes to /login?callbackUrl=/my-products. Sign-in offers Google or email; form fields are hidden until sign-in.
