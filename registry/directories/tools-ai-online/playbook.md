# Tools AI Online

- Open https://www.tools-ai.online/submit-tool.
- Free AI-tool submission is available. The required screenshot is 1280×720 or 16:9, up to 5 MB; optional icon is recommended at 512×512 PNG or SVG, up to 2 MB.
- Choose up to 3 categories and 5 tags. Pricing and contact email are required.
- Optional featured placement costs $5/month, $10/3 months, or $20/12 months.
- A reCAPTCHA widget is present; it did not reject the research browser. Review timing is not stated.
