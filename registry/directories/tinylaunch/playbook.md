# TinyLaunch

- Visit https://www.tinylaunch.com/submit - redirects to login first.
- Sign up with email, Google, GitHub, or X (𝕏/Twitter).
- Provide product URL (URL-first, after login).
- Free launch includes: homepage listing for multiple days, standard queue; a badge and do-follow backlink are guaranteed only if you rank top 3.
- Paid options: Premium Launch $39/launch (guaranteed backlink + skip queue + mid-feed placement), Featured Spot $30/week, Submission Service $279 (110+ directories incl. SourceForge, GeekWire, G2).
- Live listings at /launch/{id}-{slug} - the numeric id prefix is not predictable from the product name.
- The logo/icon field is auto-filled from the site (usually a page screenshot). Replace it with a real square logo (Edit Startup > Logo, a `input[type=file]` upload, then "Update Startup"), or moderation rejects the launch ("screenshot rather than a dedicated icon").
- Resubmission after rejection: fix the startup at /dashboard/startups/{id}/edit, then on the Startups list click "Launch", pick a date, choose Standard Free with "None" for the add-ons, press "Schedule Free Launch", and on the upsell dialog choose "Continue with free launch". It goes back to pending review (under 24 hours). Login is an emailed 6-digit code (valid 1 hour).
