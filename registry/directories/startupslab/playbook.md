# Startups Lab

- Weekly launch board, not a permanent plain listing: new batch every Monday, live community upvoting, homepage shows 'Week NN · Live now'.
- /submit only asks for Product Name + Product URL, then 'Generate Launch Listing' runs an AI scraper to prefill tagline/category/description/logo -- review and edit before it goes live. No manual moderation queue; auto-published per schedule.
- Free tier: standard queue, launches next available Monday, up to 2 active free submissions per account, no guaranteed dofollow backlink (unless you land in the week's top 3 by upvotes, which grants one automatically).
- Paid upsells: Skip the Queue ($9, guaranteed DR56 dofollow backlink + pick any Monday) and Spotlight ($29/30 days, homepage placement below hero, only 3 concurrent slots). Neither is required for a free listing.
- Account required to manage/track launches. New signups: 'Continue with Google' only (auto-creates account). The email+password form on /login is explicitly 'EXISTING EMAIL ACCOUNTS ONLY' -- not a real signup path.
- Live listings sit at https://startupslab.site/product/{slug} (verified via /product/seedancetips).
