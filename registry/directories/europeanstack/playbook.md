# EuropeanStack

- Open https://www.europeanstack.io/en/submit-product. The public form does not require an account.
- Required: product name, short description, country of origin, and category. The website, tags, markdown details, and contact/reviewer fields are optional.
- Italy is an available country. Relevant categories include Marketing Technology and Web Analytics.
- The site says submissions are reviewed before publication; no review timing is stated. No fee or payment step appears on the public form.
- The submit form displayed an ALTCHA verification widget; it had not rejected the browser during research.
