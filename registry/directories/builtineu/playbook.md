# BuiltInEu

- Open https://builtineu.eu/submit. The public form does not require an account.

- Required fields: product name, company name, country, website, category, relationship to the product, and contact email.
- Optional fields include the US product it replaces, description, and pricing model.
- The directory says review takes 1–5 business days. No listing fee is shown; sponsorship is a separate option.
