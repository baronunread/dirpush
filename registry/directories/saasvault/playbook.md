# SaaSVault

- Use the distribution page and choose the free listing option. The CTA leads to /auth/login; signup is available.
- Sign-in offers Google, GitHub, or email/password.
- The listing form is behind authentication; public copy says it asks for name, link, category, and pricing.
- Human review is required. The free tier includes directory listing, category/search placement, a nofollow link, and an editable listing URL. Optional paid upgrades are shown, but checkout is currently marked unavailable.
