# Tool Finder

- The .co host redirects to toolfinder.com. Open /submit.
- A one-time $29 Tool Listing is the base option; Vendor Pro is $79/year and includes a homepage week. No free plan is offered.
- Submission is a desktop flow. Entering an email sends a continuation link; no account is needed. The form is seen by a human, usually within 72 hours.
- Fields: tool name, 32-character quick summary, website, logo (PNG/JPG/WebP, max 500 KB), platforms, audience size, up to three categories, email, and Terms agreement.
