# JustHunt

- Public submit page: https://justhunt.co/submit. It begins with the product website URL and says it fetches title, description, and logo. After pressing Continue, sign-in is required and progress is saved; stop there unless a submission is authorized.
- Sign-in offers email/password or Google, with an account creation link.
- Pricing: free launch includes homepage placement for one week and a do-follow backlink. 25 free slots per week, first come, first served.
- Optional paid placement: Featured Spot $19, Boosted $9.99 per week, Featured $19.99 per week. If free slots are full, a paid slot unlock is $2.99. A separate Launch Boost directory-submission service starts at $39.
- Screenshot support and post-login fields were not visible in the public flow.
