# Prouct

- The English wizard is at /en/submit; choose Digital product / SaaS or Physical product. The first step asks for product type, name, website, tagline, category, description, and optional tags.
- Later steps include a logo, optional 16:9 cover, up to five screenshots, optional video URL; pricing model and starting price; company details; social links; feature/use-case/pros/cons text; and submitter contact. Complete all fields from canonical facts only.
- Publishing prompts for a free email/password account, terms acceptance, contact details, and privacy-policy consent. The account form resumes the saved listing draft afterward; reattach logo/screenshots if the browser drops them during signup.
- The public page and FAQ say submissions are free and immediate and advertise an inbound backlink to the product website. No reciprocal backlink, badge, or payment is required.
Keep the follow-up todo open and verify the public route before treating it as usable traffic.
