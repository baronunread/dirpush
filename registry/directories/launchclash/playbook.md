# LaunchClash

- Weekly launch-board format ('Discover This Week's Best Product Launches', a 'Winners' ranking tab), not a static permanent directory - listings are tagged 'Launched This Week'.
- /submit immediately asks to 'Log in with Google' (or create an email account) - no visible badge/backlink requirement or paywall before or after.
- No pricing/premium tier discoverable anywhere on the site (no /pricing route, no nav/footer link); the homepage 'Featured products'/'Sponsors' sections just carry a third-party ad, not a self-serve paid-listing option.
- Broad categories, not niche to AI or SaaS specifically.
