Visit https://startupeurope.net and click 'Submit a startup', which opens a Sign Up form: first/last name, email + password, or 'login with google'. Create a free account, then fill in startup name, country, and category. Submit your listing - no badge/backlink required to go live. You can optionally earn a Startup Europe certified badge by completing your profile (an SEO/reward perk, not a submission condition). Listing URLs use a numeric id (?id=NNNNN), not a predictable slug.
