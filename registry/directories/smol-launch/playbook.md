# Smol Launch

- /pricing is the clearest entry point; 'Submit for free' button (ref on Free Verified card) starts the free flow.
- Free Verified ($0): requires adding the Smol Launch badge to your site to verify the listing; only 50 slots/week platform-wide, and the next open free week showed a queue of ~300 makers ahead — expect a long wait.
- Premium ($19/launch): no badge, permanent DR73 backlink, launch any open week with no queue.
- Premium+ ($39): adds a one-week Featured Spot + newsletter feature.
- Launch Everywhere ($99+): done-for-you submission to 30-100+ other directories, stackable with Premium/Premium+.
- Maker Pass ($99/year) covers Premium launches for a year for repeat/serial launchers.
- General SaaS/startup directory, not AI-only; has extensive free guides/tools section unrelated to submission.
