# DevHunt

- /submit immediately redirects to a login wall; sign up with GitHub or Google (no email option).
- It's a weekly voting launch board (like Product Hunt for dev tools), not a set-and-forget listing.
- Free path: join the queue, ~6 month average wait for your launch week.
- $49 to skip the queue and pick your launch week (with a video slot); $397 for a newsletter feature ad.
- Scope: open source tools, APIs/SDKs, frameworks/libraries, IDEs/editors, testing tools, monitoring/perf tools.
- Live listing pages are at /tool/{slug} (e.g. /tool/daytona).
