# DevHunt

- /submit now returns 404. Start at https://devhunt.org/account/tools/new and sign in with GitHub or Google (no email option).
- On the first step choose "Fill it in manually" to skip the URL-first step.
- The free listing is NOFOLLOW. Dofollow is a paid upgrade from $19; a boosted launch is $49. Do not pay.
- Trap: clicking a category suggestion button submits the whole form and jumps to /account/tools/activate-launch/<slug>?new=1. Pick the category with the keyboard, and press submit only after the form is complete.
- Untick the "answer questions with AI" checkbox.
- A leftover "Add banner" modal from a previous tool can cover the page; close it with its Close button.
- After creation, choose "Keep my free spot" (free). The free launch slot queue is about 5 years out (a slot picked on 2026-11-01 was Oct 21, 2031).
- It is a weekly voting launch board, not a set-and-forget listing.
- Scope: open source tools, APIs/SDKs, frameworks/libraries, IDEs/editors, testing tools, monitoring/perf tools.
- Live listing pages are at /tool/{slug}.
