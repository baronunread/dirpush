- Free Starter Listing: basic listing included unconditionally, nofollow link by default, becomes dofollow only if you embed their badge; ~24h processing. The badge is not required to be listed, only for dofollow SEO value.

- $10/week Featured Listing: homepage placement for 7 days, limited to 8 concurrent spots.
- Pricing page moved to /get-featured (old /pricing now 404s).
- Live listings sit at /startups/{slug} (e.g. /startups/searchless-ai).
