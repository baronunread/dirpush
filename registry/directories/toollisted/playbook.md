# ToolListed

- /submit redirects to a login page: email+password, Google, or GitHub.
- Free plan: add a backlink to toollisted.com on your site, then wait for manual approval; no backlink means 6+ month queue.
- Pro $9.90 one-time skips the queue and no backlink needed.
- Listing URLs are /item/{slug} but some slugs include the domain, so not derivable.
