# TrustRadius Vendor Listing

1. Go to https://trustradius.com/vendors, open nav 'Vendor solutions' > 'How we help vendors', or go straight to https://solutions.trustradius.com/claim-your-profile/.
2. Click 'CLAIM MY FREE PROFILE' (there are two on the page; either works) -- routes to auth.trustradius.com/login (Auth0).
3. Sign up with email/password or 'Sign in with LinkedIn' -- no Google or GitHub option despite what earlier notes said.
4. After auth, flow lands in the Vendor Portal (vendor.trustradius.com/vendor) to build the profile: company/product name, description, website, logo, features, integrations, pricing info, security info.
5. Free Profile page mentions 'Qualify for awards and badges that boost your SEO, conversions, and brand' -- this is TrustRadius awarding you a badge as a free-tier perk, not something you must add anywhere first. No badge/backlink requirement gates the free listing.
6. Premium Profile is a separate paid upsell pitched throughout (review-generation campaigns, downstream intent data, competitive analytics) -- decline it to stay free.
7. The main /vendors search page is behind Cloudflare's 'Esecuzione della verifica di sicurezza' (Italian-localized) interstitial; it cleared once with a headed browser + an explicit click on the verification checkbox, but reverted on a later reload. Expect to retry headed if scripting this.
