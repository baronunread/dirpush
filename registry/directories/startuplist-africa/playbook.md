# StartupList Africa

- /submit-startup offers two paths: 'Submit a new startup' (guided ~8 min flow) or 'Claim or update a profile' (search first to avoid duplicates).
- Requires creating an account to save progress; nothing goes live until the submitter approves the draft and the team verifies it. No badge/backlink requirement.

- Focused on African startups/ecosystems specifically (funding data, investors, market pulse).
