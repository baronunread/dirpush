# SourceForge Business Software Directory

- No account/login needed - just fill the request form at /software/vendors/new and submit; SourceForge reviews it manually.

- Dismiss the cookie-consent dialog first.
- Required: your name, email, company, title, website, software title (52 chars), logo upload (square, hi-res), product description (1000 chars), audience, category.
- Optional: phone, company founded year, company location, starting price, free-version/free-trial checkboxes, support/training options, supported platforms.
- No badge/backlink requirement anywhere on the form to submit or go live.
- Premium marketing packages exist for extra visibility but pricing isn't shown on this page and are not required to get the free listing.
