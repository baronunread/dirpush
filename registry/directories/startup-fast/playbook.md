- Real submit page is https://www.startupfa.st/submit (old /submit-startup 404s). Also reachable via 'Submit Product' nav / 'Submit Now' hero button.
- Runs on the open-source Open-Launch platform. Free tier ($0) legally REQUIRES displaying a dofollow 'Powered by Open-Launch' badge on every page of your site (see /legal/badges) — this is a condition of using the free launch, not just an SEO bonus, so treat as CONDITIONAL_FREE.

- Premium ($19/launch): no badge required, guaranteed dofollow backlink, 30-day launch window, 12 slots/day.
- Sign up via Google, GitHub, or email+password at /sign-up (Cloudflare Turnstile present but not blocking).
