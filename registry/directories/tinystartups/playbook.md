# Tiny Startups

- Submit at https://www.tinystartups.com/submit. The five-step wizard starts with the product URL and autofetches the name and description.
- The product details step includes name, tagline, category, three differentiators, optional longer description, email, logo, and cover image. The site says no signup is needed; an email associates the launch with an account and opts into the weekly newsletter.
- No free listing anymore. After the form, the only way to go live is a paid £69 "Instant listing".
Do not submit it again unless the user asks for a second launch.
