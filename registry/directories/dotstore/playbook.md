# dotstore

- Form at /submit has steps Basics, Media, Details, Launch, Review; draft autosaves. Name max 40, tagline max 60; pricing radios Free/Freemium/Paid/Open source; many categories (Productivity, Design, Developer Tools, AI, ...).
- Launch step: choose Page (fast-track into the store after editor check) or Board (weekly launch board, votes all week, top three keep a badge).
- Sign-in required to upload images and submit; exact providers not confirmed.
- Listing URLs for launches contain the week (e.g. /launches/2026-w45/chattr), so no stable pattern.
