# SaaSFame

- Sibling sites under same operator (Hyhor): ProductFame, aitoolfame.com, toolfame.com, directoryfame.com — likely same submission mechanics, worth checking separately.
- /submit redirects to /auth/login if not signed in (email/password, Google, GitHub).
- Free tier: 1 dofollow link, must keep badge forever or gets unpublished, up to 180-day review, may be rejected outright.
- Paid Basic ($14.9) skips badge requirement and lists immediately.
