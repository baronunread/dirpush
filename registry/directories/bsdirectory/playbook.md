# BSDirectory

- Open /submit. It is one form: enter name, URL, short description (20–220 characters), founder name, contact email, category, and product type. Logo and further details are optional.
- Keep the free standard plan. Featured costs $4.99 once for priority review and a 30-day spotlight; do not choose it.
- "Submit for review" is the final submit; there is no later plan step. Success text: "Your product has been submitted."
- A private management link is shown after submit. It contains a token; do not record it.
- No account or card is needed for the free listing. Every submission is reviewed before publication.
