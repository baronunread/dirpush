Go to /products and click 'ADD YOUR PRODUCT' (top of Products Database section) or 'Add Yours' in the sidebar. Requires signing in with a Google account (Indie Hackers has no email/password signup). No badge or backlink required to submit or go live. Listings show name, tagline, and self-reported monthly revenue; filterable by category and business model. Product pages live at indiehackers.com/product/{slug}.
