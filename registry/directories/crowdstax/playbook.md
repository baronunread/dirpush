# Crowdstax

- Submit at https://www.crowdstax.com/submit. The site requires an account; login offers Google or GitHub. The product fields are hidden until sign-in.
- Free community submissions receive manual review, launch-feed placement, and a product-page backlink after approval; no payment, credit card, instant publishing, or guaranteed feature is required.
- Optional paid plans: Pro $19.99 for instant publication and 15.5 days featured; Sponsor $49.99 for premium placement and broader visibility.
- No form fields were recorded because the flow gates them behind login. No submission was sent.
