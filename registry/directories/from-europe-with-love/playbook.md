# From Europe, With Love

- Submit at https://www.fromeuropewithlove.eu/en/submit. The open first step asks tool name, website URL, headquarters country/city, category/subcategory, and a short company/product description. The form has three steps; later fields were not inspected.
- Listings are free. Sponsorship is separate and labeled. The site reviews entries against published criteria.
- Company headquarters and any parent/holding company headquarters must be in the EU or UK.
