# EuropeanApps

- Send a submission to hello@europeanapps.com with the app/service name and country where the company is actually based. Optionally add one sentence about where it falls short.
- The standard queue is free; priority verification is €99 and guarantees review/publication within 7 days.
- Strict scope: personal-use apps and services only. The site explicitly excludes marketing, CRM, website, hosting, and online-store products. The optional badge is free after listing; the homepage sponsor slot is €99/month and is separate from listing.
