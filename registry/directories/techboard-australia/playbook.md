# Techboard

- Add a company through https://techboard.com.au/get-listed/add-a-company/; an account is required.
- Free registration is at https://techboard.com.au/create-account/. The account page offers email registration (first name, last name, email, reCAPTCHA) and social login with Apple, Facebook, Google, LinkedIn, or X.
- Techboard only targets Australian startups and young technology companies. Its rules require Australian founding or relocation of headquarters plus significant operations to Australia, and the team screens applications. The profile form is behind sign-in.
