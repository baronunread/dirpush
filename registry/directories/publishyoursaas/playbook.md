# PublishYourSaaS

- /submit works without an account (just email) for a manually-reviewed listing; optionally sign in with Google/GitHub for a self-service dashboard with relaunch tools.
- Free tier badge is optional and only affects dofollow vs nofollow status - confirmed live text: 'Nofollow link without a verified badge' - not a hard requirement to be listed at all; checked every 24 hours.
- Free manual review takes 2-3 weeks; there's also a separate paid 'manual submission' path reviewed within 24 hours via Whop payment.
- Relaunches cost extra on paid tiers ($5.99 Starter, $19.99 Advanced) but are free for account-managed free listings.
