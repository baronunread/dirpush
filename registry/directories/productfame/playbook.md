# ProductFame

- Sister site of SaaSFame/ToolFame/AIToolFame.
- 4-step submit: product info, assets (logo + product image), plan and launch date, review.
- Free plan: needs Ahrefs DR >= 5 and the ProductFame badge kept on your site; otherwise pick Basic $6.9.
- Auth methods: only an 'Email' option was seen on the sign-in page; Google/GitHub not confirmed.
- Listing URLs are slug plus numeric suffix (e.g. /products/best-launch-platforms-2), so no pattern.
