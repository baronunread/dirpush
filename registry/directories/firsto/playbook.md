# Firsto

- Navigate to https://firsto.co and sign up (email/Google/GitHub)
- Click 'Submit a Project' to launch
- Choose plan: Free (180 day wait), Premium ($19.9, 24h), or Pro ($59.9, featured)
- Fill product details: name, description, logo, website, categories
- Manual review required
- Free tier has persistent product page even after launch day
- Premium/Pro get SEO backlinks and homepage features
- Project pages live at firsto.co/projects/{slug}
