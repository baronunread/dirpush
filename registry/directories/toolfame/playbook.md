# ToolFame

- Broad tools directory (not AI-only) with ~1.68M monthly visitors claimed and DR 76.
- Free tier is heavily deprioritized: 180-day review window, may be rejected, and requires keeping their badge live permanently (they recheck and unpublish if it's removed) - only 1 dofollow link.
- Paid Basic ($14.9, one-time) removes the badge requirement entirely and lists immediately with 3 dofollow links; Pro ($49.9) adds featured placement and 5 links.
- $79.9 Guest Post add-on bundles Pro plus a blog post (you supply the article) with up to 2 more contextual dofollow links.
