# HighTechAttic

- Listings are posted from a free account; the site allows up to 10 listings per account.
- The public add-project link routes to /dashboard/projects/new and redirects signed-out visitors to /auth/signin?next=/dashboard/projects/new. Google and email/password sign-in are visible, along with a free signup link.
- The listing form is behind authentication; fields and media requirements were not visible without creating an account.
- This is a collaboration board with public listings and Wanted/Offer asks, not a timed launch leaderboard. Optional sidebar ads are separate from listings.
