# Squidir

- The homepage says listings are free for European SaaS. Start with List your product at https://squidir.com/account/register.
- Registration offers Google or email/password and asks for name, email, password, and confirmation. The listing editor is behind the account gate; no account was created during research.
- Paid Verified (€49/month) and Analytics (€149/month) plans are optional; Squidir states payment does not affect product ranking. A European SaaS origin is required.
