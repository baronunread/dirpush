# Enlisted

- Maker submissions are free and reviewed by a person. Submit at https://app.enlisted.directory/submit after signing in.
- The public submit route redirects to a sign-in screen; sign-in providers and post-login fields were not visible while signed out.
- Enlisted’s 200+ GitHub-star threshold applies to automatic imports of open-source tools, not its separate maker-submission route.
A claim only adds the verified-owner badge.
- Manual review time and media fields are not stated.
