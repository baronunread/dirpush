# Enlisted

- Maker submissions are free and reviewed by a person. Submit at https://app.enlisted.directory/submit after signing in.
- The form requires a "Listed on Enlisted" badge in the footer of the product site. Add it, deploy, then click the Verify badge button; Submit stays locked until verification passes. Needs backlink permission.
- The 200+ GitHub-star threshold applies to automatic imports of open-source tools, not to maker submissions.
- A claim only adds the verified-owner badge.
- Review time is not stated.
