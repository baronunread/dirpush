# SaaStore

- Select 'List your app for free'; it opens the seller signup flow.
- Seller account signup asks for full name, email, and password; Google sign-in is also available.
- The FAQ says product listing is free, with no sales commission; optional premium placement may be offered in the future.
- Sellers submit through a wizard and listings receive manual review. SaaStore focuses on web-based software, including SaaS, web apps, and browser extensions; products remain on the seller's own site. The wizard's product fields were not visible before account creation.
