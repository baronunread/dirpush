# Favors.dev

- Submit at https://favors.dev/projects/new. The form is open through the product details; creating an account is required at the final publish step.
- Required fields: website URL, name (60 chars max), tagline (100 chars max), category, and About text (at least 40 characters). A screenshot is optional (PNG/JPEG/WebP, max 5 MB); launch date and FAQ are optional.
- AI autofill reads the product URL and drafts name, tagline, description, category, and screenshot. Review the draft for accuracy.
- Publishing is free. The default review is two weeks; completing one favor or adding the Favors.dev badge makes publication instant. The site has a founder-to-founder favor exchange, so decide whether to participate before choosing the instant path.
