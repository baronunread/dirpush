# Neeed Directory

- Visit https://neeed.directory/submit
- Two plans shown: Free ($0, 'with our badge') or Premium ($19 one-time).
- Sign in (email/password, or Google) is required before either plan's form.
- Fill in product details: name, URL, category, description.
- Free tier: the final submit step is a modal 'Add our badge to your site' - the Free listing needs the badge on your site, and the ONLY submit button reads 'I'll add the badge - Submit'; the team verifies the badge is actually present afterward. This is a real requirement to go live, not just a dofollow perk, despite the landing page's softer wording ('Dofollow backlink once the badge is verified').
- Premium tier: pay $19 once for a permanent dofollow backlink with no badge requirement, an enhanced profile (highlights, who it's for, FAQ, FAQ schema), and 7 days in 'Just launched' on the homepage/category top.
- No screenshot uploads in submission.
- Guidelines: accepts software (web/mobile/SaaS/dev tools/extensions/APIs) and digital products (templates/courses/ebooks); rejects adult content and local service businesses.
