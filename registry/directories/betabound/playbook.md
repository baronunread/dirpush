# Betabound

- Only fits products that are currently in an active beta with their own landing page (not a released/GA product) - Betabound explicitly rejects sign-up or free-trial pages.
- Fill the announcement form: product name, brief one-line description (120 chars), Beta URL (your own landing page explaining how to join/give feedback), product type, optional requirements/incentives, expiration date.
- No account/signup needed; team manually reviews and writes the post themselves (not self-serve), emails you a draft for approval, a few business days turnaround.
- No way to pay for a post or guaranteed placement; free listing goes to homepage + social media, no badge or backlink required to go live.
- Betabound doesn't collect tester feedback for you - manage that yourself or via Centercode (a separate paid platform).
