# Huzzler

- Nav has 'Submit product'; page /submit-product shows a Free vs Launch Boost comparison table.
- Free ($0): requires displaying a Huzzler badge on your site, goes live after ~72 day queue, not indexed by Google, not shown on homepage, but still gets a DR65+ dofollow backlink and its own product page.
- Launch Boost ($39 one-time): no badge required, live immediately, indexed by Google, shown in 'Recently Boosted' on homepage.
- Clicking 'Submit your startup' routes to /login (email/password or 'Login with Google'; 'Register here' for new accounts) before you can fill the submission form.
- All submissions manually reviewed by a person to filter spam.
