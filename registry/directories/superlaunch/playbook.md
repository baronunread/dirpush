# Super Launch

- Daily launch board with leaderboard and upvotes, medals for top 3 each cycle.
- Click 'Launch Now', which routes through accounts.superlaun.ch for signup (Google OAuth or email+password via Clerk).
- Paste product URL to autofetch name/description/logo, pick a category, add tagline and description.
- Every product gets 7 days of front-page visibility for free, no conditions.
- Optional $49 one-time ad slot (max 10/month) via /advertise for extra visibility -- unrelated to the free launch.
