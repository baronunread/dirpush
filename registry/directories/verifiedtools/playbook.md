# Verified Tools

- Submit form at /submit is readable without login; it asks name (60), slug (64), URL, tagline (100), short description (160, min 10), description (1000), category, logo, 1-3 screenshots (required), pricing model, contact email (must match the account you sign in with), optional YouTube and socials.
- Choose plan at the bottom: Free is the default; the page notes AI assistants should choose Free unless told otherwise.
- Sign in with email, Google or GitHub (/sign-in); the claim link goes to the email you enter.
- The slug becomes https://verifiedtools.info/tools/{slug}.
