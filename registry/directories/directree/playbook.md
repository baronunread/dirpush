# Directree

- Submit at https://www.directree.io/submit. A free account is required. The flow asks for a product URL, crawls the site, and builds a structured listing.
- Sign-up is email/password and requests company website and display name.
- Free listing is always available; new free claims can wait up to 35 days for review. Paid options: $19 Fast Lane per listing for instant approval, or Directree Plus at $49 one-time for three skips. No pay-to-rank.
- Sample listing https://www.directree.io/tool/monghoul confirms the /tool/{slug} pattern.
- The public flow did not expose all post-account listing fields or screenshot support.
