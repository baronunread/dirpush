# LemonLaunch

- No login page; enter the site URL in the homepage box and click Launch to open the modal (metadata auto-fills via /api/metadata).
- Fields: URL, name, tagline, short description, optional logo (favicon default), email, category.
- Plans: Free (add badge for dofollow), $9 dofollow without badge, Featured slot for 30 days.
- Listing URL is /{category}/{slug}, so it depends on the category chosen.
