Application at sell.appsumo.com takes ~5 minutes. Only ~10% of applicants are accepted (evaluated on functionality, partner stability, market fit, value). No cash cost to apply, but AppSumo takes a revenue share on every sale made through the platform - there's no free-listing path. Most partners run lifetime deals, but annual/other pricing structures are also negotiable. A 'Select' badge (premium placement) is earned, not purchased directly.
