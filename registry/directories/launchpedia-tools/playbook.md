# LaunchPedia

- launchpedia.co's main homepage is a resource/affiliate hub linking to ~155 other directories (Uneed, Startup Stash, Startup Ranking, etc. via affiliate/ref links) plus a paid done-for-you launch service - it is not itself a submission form.
- Its own directory lives at /launchpedia-tools/ ('LaunchPedia Tools'); 'Submit your Tool' there routes off-site to a Gumroad checkout ($9), and only after payment do you receive the actual submission form.
- No free listing option found anywhere.
