# LaunchIgniter

- Free tier requires adding LaunchIgniter badge to product homepage; Cloudflare may block verification (offer manual confirmation option).
- Free launches: manual review before going live, date not guaranteed, limited dofollow backlinks (top 3 only get DR 75).
- Paid launches ($12-15): no review queue, guaranteed launch date, instant availability.
- Paste product URL to auto-fill name, description, and logo.
- All launches featured for 1 week on homepage; top 3 weekly products get Winner badge.
- Pro tier automatically submits to 2 partner directories (StartupTrusted, SaaSGrow) for extra backlinks.
- Community feedback available during live week.
