# Launch Llama

- Submission entry point is a URL box on the homepage ('AI prefill', 'takes 30 secs') or /submit directly; /submit requires signing in first (Google or email+password).
- Free plan is genuinely free forever, but takes ~4 weeks to be reviewed and published; pay $39 one-time ('Instant Launch') to go live immediately and get a dofollow backlink, or $23/week for the ongoing Featured tier.
- A '/badge' page offers an embeddable 'Featured on Launch Llama' badge, but it's purely optional self-promotion (its own copy: 'Show the world you launched...'), not required to submit or go live.
- Same operator/partner network as ScrollLaunch and Nick Launches (cross-promoted in the footer).
- No internal listing page pattern found - product cards link straight to each product's own external site.
