# SubmitMySaas

- Weekly launch cohort model: products launch together every Monday 8am UTC; only 10 free slots/week.
- Free listing itself has no condition; only the dofollow backlink is conditional (top-3 weekly rank or display their badge).
- $19 Premium Launch bypasses the free-slot cap, guarantees the backlink, and gets priority placement.
- Submit form requires login (email+password w/ Cloudflare Turnstile, or Google/GitHub OAuth) at /sign-in?redirect=/projects/submit.
