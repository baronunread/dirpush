# Find Your SaaS

- Old /list-your-saas URL is dead (404). Submit via https://findyoursaas.com/pricing -> 'Submit Your Tool' button.
- That button goes straight to Google OAuth sign-in (accounts.google.com) - Google account required, no email/password signup seen.
- Standard ($0, promo price, normally $15): lifetime listing, no backlink, 72h review, no badge required.
- Featured ($9/mo, normally $30): featured tag, priority placement, analytics, lifetime backlink, social/newsletter promo, AI summary.
- FAQ says the $0 tier is capped at '100 free listings for a limited time' - worth re-checking it's still open before relying on it.
