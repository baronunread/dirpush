# HuntScreens

- Submit at https://huntscreens.com/submit
- Choose pricing tier:
  - Queue: $0, ~30 days review (free path) - button reads 'Submit for Free'
  - VIP Pass: $10, 24h review - button reads 'Pay $10.00 & Submit'
- Optional add-ons: $79 SEO backlink (do-follow, permanent, purely a paid purchase - no badge involved), $29-99 sidebar ads (7-30 days), bundle packs
- Requires product URL + checking a refund-policy acknowledgement box
- No login required to reach/fill the submit form itself; login modal (Google, GitHub, or email) is used elsewhere on the site (e.g. dashboard access)
- Manual review; rejected products get full refund, no refund once published
- Live listings at /products/{slug} but some slugs get an appended random number for uniqueness (e.g. musegen-186) - not a reliable pattern to predict
