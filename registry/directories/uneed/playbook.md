# Uneed

- Sign up with email
- Submit product info, logo, and description
- Choose launch plan: Free 'Join the line' (up to 5 months wait) or pay $14.99 (Fast-track, ~14 days) / $29.99 (Skip the line, choose date)
- IMPORTANT: the free tier requires an upvote score of 10 to stay published — if the product doesn't reach that after launch, the listing does not remain up. 20 upvotes unlocks a dofollow backlink from a 75DR domain.
- Paid tiers reduce the vote-threshold risk (Fast-track still needs 10 upvotes for dofollow but Skip the line does not mention a stay-published requirement).
- Separate 'Uneed Pro' subscription ($99-139/yr) is a community perk bundle, unrelated to whether a product gets listed.
