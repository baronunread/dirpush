# Submit AI Tools - Free Listing

1. Visit https://submitaitools.org/
2. Click 'List Your Tool Free' or 'Start free submission'
3. Sign in with Google or email
4. On your dashboard, get the required backlink URL
5. Add the backlink to your website (keep it active for listing to remain live)
6. Verify the backlink in the dashboard
7. Complete submission form:
   - Tool Name
   - Tagline
   - Email
   - Website URL
   - Pricing type (Free, Freemium, Free Trial, Paid, Subscription)
   - App platform (Web App, Mobile App, Extension, etc.)
8. Submit for review
9. Review completes within 5 business days

**Important:** Backlink must remain active on your website for the listing to stay live. Paid upgrades (Basic $19 lifetime, Pro $99/month, Spotlight $999/month) offer additional visibility and features.
